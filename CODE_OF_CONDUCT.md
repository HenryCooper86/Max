# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Read it there. It applies to every space where the project is discussed: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and assume good faith.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.

## Reporting

To report behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's
**Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) or by a direct message to a maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in an incident will not take part in handling it.

Maintainers may remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow the code of conduct.
